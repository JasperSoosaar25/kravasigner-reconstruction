# Code of Conduct

This community welcomes newcomers and people with different levels of experience. Be respectful, explain your ideas clearly and give useful feedback about the work.

- Critique code and behavior without insulting or harassing people.
- Respect privacy. Do not post anyone's credentials, personal information or private app data.
- Avoid spam, trolling, threats, discriminatory language and piracy.
- Disagree constructively and give contributors room to learn.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. Use GitHub's reporting controls for abusive content. For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
